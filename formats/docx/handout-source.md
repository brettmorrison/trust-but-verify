# TRUST BUT VERIFY

**You don't have to get suspicious of everybody. You add one step.**

---

## BEFORE ANY MONEY MOVES

**1. Look up the number yourself.**
Not the number they gave you. The one on your card or your statement.

**2. Call the person yourself.**
Hang up first. If it was real, they'll still be there.

**3. Wait a day.**
Real problems survive a night's sleep. Scams don't.

---

## THREE SIGNS TO STOP

- **It came to you.** You didn't start it.
- **It moved you fast.** Fear, worry, or a deadline.
- **It wants a transfer.** Money, a code, or your computer.

---

## NEVER, NOT ONCE, NOT EVER

- Nobody legitimate is paid in gift cards.
- Your bank will never ask you to move money out of your bank.
- Never read a text code out loud. Not to anyone.
- Nobody comes to your home to collect cash, gold, or valuables.
- Nobody legitimate calls you and asks for access to your computer.

---

## THE FAMILY CODE WORD

Pick one word your family knows and nobody else could guess. Never post it
anywhere. When a frightened voice calls asking for money, ask for the word.

Four minutes at a family meal. It defeats every voice-cloning scam there is.
