# Privacy policy

**We don't collect anything that identifies you, don't sell or share
anything, and don't use cookies.**

## What we don't do

No cookies. No accounts or logins. No mailing list. No ads or ad trackers.
No selling or sharing data — there's none to sell.

## What we do collect

**Anonymous pageview counts**, via Cloudflare Web Analytics — cookie-free,
no script (this site's CSP blocks scripts entirely). It shows which pages
get read. It can't connect a visit to a person or a return visit.

**If you use the [feedback form](/feedback)**, what you type is emailed to
the maintainer to read and reply to — not stored in a database or added to
any list. Prefer email? **translations [at] trustbutverifyproject [dot] org** works
the same way.

**Standard server logs** — Cloudflare briefly logs technical request data
(like IP address) for security, same as nearly every site online. We don't
use it for anything about you individually.

## Why

A site about not letting scammers take your information can't quietly take
yours either, even with good intentions. So we don't collect what we don't
need, rather than collect it and promise to be careful.

## Questions

If this doesn't match what the site actually does, that's a bug — tell us.
**translations [at] trustbutverifyproject [dot] org**, or open an issue on
**[GitHub](https://github.com/brettmorrison/trust-but-verify)**.
