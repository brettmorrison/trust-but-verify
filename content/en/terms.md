## This content is AI-generated

Most of the writing on this site — the English pages, the translations, the
print materials — was produced with the help of AI tools, directed and
reviewed by the project's human maintainer. It is not written or reviewed by
a lawyer, a licensed financial advisor, or law enforcement, and it should not
be treated as legal, financial, or investigative advice.

We work to keep every fact accurate and every statistic correctly sourced and
dated (see each page for its FBI IC3 / FTC citations). AI-assisted writing
can still contain mistakes that a careful human read might have caught. If
you find one, we want to know:
**translations@trustbutverifyproject.org**

## Translations are not validated until we say so

**Every non-English page, fridge sheet, and wallet card is machine-translated
and has not been checked by a native speaker, unless that page explicitly
says otherwise.** Each one carries a warning band, in its own language,
saying so. Do not treat an unvalidated translation as equivalent to the
English original, and please don't print or hand out an unvalidated piece
until a native speaker has reviewed it — see
**[help us validate a language](/help-translate)**.

Once a native speaker has reviewed a page, its status changes and the
warning band comes off. Until then, assume the translation may contain
errors, awkward phrasing, or — worst case — an inaccuracy in something that
matters, like a phone number or an instruction.
