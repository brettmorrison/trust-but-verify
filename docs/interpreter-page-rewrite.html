Why it stops at 7.7 rather than 6.3

Getting below the site median would mean replacing terms the page cannot afford to lose — “Area Agency on Aging”, “mutual assistance associations”, “refugee resettlement agencies” are the actual names a caller has to ask for. Those are multi-syllable by necessity, and they hold the grade up. What was fixable was sentence length: the longest sentence went from 53 words to 33, and the page averages 11.8 words per sentence instead of 16.2.
