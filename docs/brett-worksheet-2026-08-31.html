10. Add a DMARC record

You~5 minafter item 1

Without it there is no enforced policy when a spoofed @trustbutverifyproject.org message fails SPF/DKIM. Do this after item 1, so SPF and DKIM are both live first.

Open Cloudflare DNS

Type:    TXT
Name:    _dmarc
Content: v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:translations@trustbutverifyproject.org

p=quarantine rather than p=reject on purpose: it sends failing mail to spam instead of bouncing it, which is safer while this domain's mail flow is new. Reassess after a few weeks of clean reports.

Regret if you skip it

A scammer can spoof mail from your domain to the exact audience trained to trust it — and impersonating the help is how recovery scams already work.
